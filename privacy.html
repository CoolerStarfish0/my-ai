Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

In plain English:

Axon processes the messages and information you choose to provide so it can respond and provide its features. Some chat messages may be stored on Axon's backend when the chat privacy notice is displayed and the feature is used. If you connect Google, Axon uses the Google permissions you approve to carry out the actions you request.

1. Who operates Axon?

Axon AI is an independently operated project. For privacy questions, contact yuvrajchoksey92@gmail.com.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: Firebase Authentication identifiers and, where provided by Google, account name and email address used for sign-in and account-related features.
  • Messages and attachments: prompts, conversation context, and images you choose to submit so Axon can answer. Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential records, or information you do not have permission to share.
  • Chat history and memory: some chat history and memory may be saved in your browser. If Axon displays a chat privacy notice and you use the consent-based chat-history feature, messages may also be stored in the backend database and accessible to the Axon operator for support/administration. Do not assume conversations are private from the operator.
  • Usage and account records: feature-use counts, rank/credit usage, and technical records needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Technical information: hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, request details, browser information, and security logs. Axon also requests approximate location information from the ipwho.is service for a site feature.
  • Google connection information: if you connect Google, Axon stores your Google account email, granted scopes, connection metadata, and an encrypted refresh token in its Firebase/Firestore backend so the connection can persist. The encryption key is configured separately on the server.

3. How Axon uses information

Information is used to provide chat and memory features, process requests, operate Google integrations, save user preferences or account state, measure aggregate feature usage, enforce usage limits, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.

4. AI processing and service providers

When you send a message, Axon sends the prompt and relevant conversation context to the AI endpoint configured for the service. The project is designed to use a separately configured local-model bridge, but that endpoint and its hosting/network path are controlled by the operator and may change. Do not assume a message stays on your own device or that all processing is exclusively local. Firebase/Google services, Vercel hosting, and other endpoints used by specific features may process information needed to provide those features under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Google integrations

Google integrations are optional. If you connect your Google account, Axon requests the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. Depending on the scopes granted and the feature you use, Axon can search eligible Drive files, read or edit Docs and Sheets, create or update Forms, view Classroom courses/rosters, list or create Calendar events, and create Gmail drafts. Axon's Gmail feature creates drafts; it is not designed to send email automatically. Actions that change Google content should be reviewed carefully and may require confirmation in the interface.

Axon uses Google data only to provide the features you request, maintain the connection, and support the operation and security of those features. Google data is processed by Axon’s backend to perform requested actions. Do not connect an account or grant permissions unless you understand and accept the access shown by Google. Google API data handling is also subject to Google's terms and policies.

You can review or revoke Axon's Google access at Google Account connections. Revoking access can disable connected features. You may also contact the operator to request deletion of stored Axon Google connection data.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Axon does not need your information for advertising. Information may be processed by service providers that host, authenticate, store, or run requested features, and may be disclosed if required by law or necessary to address abuse, security incidents, or protect rights and safety. The operator may be able to access backend records, including consent-based saved chats and account/usage records. We do not promise that information is inaccessible to the operator or service providers.

7. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the relevant feature, maintain account state, meet security needs, or comply with legal requirements. Browser-stored chats or memories can generally be removed through the browser/site controls where available. For server-stored information or Google connection data, email yuvrajchoksey92@gmail.com with the account details needed to identify your request. Some records may remain temporarily in backups or logs. We are still developing the service, so retention periods and self-service deletion tools may evolve.

8. Security

Axon uses authentication and server-side access checks, and Google refresh tokens are encrypted before storage. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Keep your Google account secure, review requested permissions, and avoid submitting sensitive information.

9. Children and age

Axon is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you are a child, use the service with a parent or guardian's guidance and do not submit personal or sensitive information without their permission. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions or deletion requests.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Axon changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. Continued use after an update means the updated policy may apply to future use; where law requires consent, we will seek it.
